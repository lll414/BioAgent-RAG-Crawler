# Appendix A — Acknowledgments

For authors and citation details, see [Appendix D](https://bioconductor.org/books/release/OSTA/pages/apx-citation.html).

The authors also thank **Aaron T. L. Lun** for feedback, advice, as well as his substantial contributions to the Bioconductor ecosystem; **Abby Spangler**, **Madhavi Tippani**, and **Brenda Pardo** for initial work and discussions on Visium data analysis workflows and preprocessing procedures carried out at the Lieber Institute for Brain Development; and members of the **Bioconductor Core Team and community** for computational assistance and advice. In addition, the authors thank readers from the Bioconductor and wider research communities for feedback, suggestions, and contributions.

* This book is built using [**BiocBook**](https://bioconductor.org/packages/release/BiocBook), developed by [Jacques Serizay](https://jserizay.com/) for building Quarto books with Bioconductor. More details on BiocBook can be found in this [demo](https://bioconductor.org/books/release/BiocBookDemo/).
* This book is inspired by the **Orchestrating Single-Cell Analysis with Bioconductor (OSCA)** book ([Amezquita et al. 2020](https://bioconductor.org/books/release/OSTA/pages/apx-acknowledgments.html#ref-Amezquita2020-OSCA)) on analysis workflows for single-cell data using R and Bioconductor, available [here](https://bioconductor.org/books/release/OSCA/).
* Details on data preprocessing procedures for **10x Genomics Visium** data can be found in our related online book [Visium data preprocessing](https://lmweber.org/Visium-data-preprocessing/).
* **Feedback and suggestions** may be provided as [GitHub](https://github.com/lmweber/OSTA) issues for discussion with the maintainers.
